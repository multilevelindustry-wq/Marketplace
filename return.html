Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

A clear process for eligible returns, refunds, replacements, and order cancellations.

Eligibility

Returns may be available for damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially misdescribed products, subject to applicable policy and law.

Special Categories

Perishable, personalized, hygiene-sensitive, digital, opened, or other special products may have different rules where legally permitted.

Return Requests

Submit eligible requests within zongo247customer.care@gmail.com from delivery or the applicable event.

How to Request

Open the order, select the return/help option, describe the problem accurately, and provide requested photos or evidence.

Item Condition

Where a return is approved, follow the required condition, packaging, accessory, and documentation requirements.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed through the applicable payment method or marketplace process. Processing times can vary.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

Report problems as soon as possible and retain parcel and product evidence where practical.

Cancellation

Cancellation may be available before an order reaches a non-cancellable stage. After dispatch, the return process may apply.

Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy removes consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.
