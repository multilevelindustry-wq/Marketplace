What Is Advertising?

Advertising is a way of giving selected products additional visibility so more potential customers can discover them.

👀

More Visibility

Your product may compete with many other products in the marketplace. Advertising can give a product additional opportunities to appear where shoppers are more likely to notice it.

🎯

Reach Relevant Shoppers

Effective advertising starts with understanding who is most likely to want your product and presenting the right product to the right audience.

📊

Learn From Performance

Advertising can help sellers understand which products attract attention and which products may need better pricing, images, descriptions or offers.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Advertising does not replace a good product.

An advertisement can help people discover your product, but discovery is only the beginning.

When shoppers reach your product page, they still need a clear product image, accurate information, competitive pricing, reliable delivery information and a trustworthy seller experience.

The strongest advertising strategy combines visibility + product quality + customer trust.

WHY ADVERTISE?

Why Should Sellers Consider Ads?

Advertising should have a purpose. Before spending money on promotion, understand what you want the advertising campaign to accomplish.

🔎

Help New Products Get Discovered

A new product may need time to gain organic visibility. Promotion can help introduce it to shoppers.

🛒

📣

Sellers can use promotion strategically when launching a new product, seasonal item, special offer or important inventory.

CHOOSE CAREFULLY

What Should You Advertise?

Do not automatically advertise every product. Start with products that are ready to convert attention into genuine customer interest.

01

Strong Product Images

Choose products with clear, attractive and honest images that show the item properly.

02

Competitive Pricing

Compare your price with similar products before deciding to promote an item.

03

Accurate Descriptions

Make sure buyers can quickly understand what the product is, what it includes and what they should expect.

04

Products You Can Fulfil

Avoid promoting products you cannot reliably supply or deliver to customers.

GET READY

Prepare Your Store Before Advertising

The best time to prepare your product listings is before advertising becomes available.

1

Complete Your Seller Profile

Give customers confidence by keeping your seller information accurate and complete.

2

Upload Better Product Photos

Use clear images with good lighting and multiple views where appropriate.

3

Improve Product Titles

Make your titles specific and useful. Avoid unnecessary words that make the product difficult to understand.

4

Write Useful Descriptions

Explain the product clearly, including important features, size, quantity, materials and other relevant information.

5

Keep Inventory Updated

Do not promote products that are unavailable or difficult to fulfil.

6

Understand Your Customer

Know who is most likely to buy your product, what they need and why they would choose it.

SELLER CHECKLIST

Advertising Do's & Don'ts

Good advertising is not just about getting attention. It is about getting the right attention and creating a trustworthy shopping experience.

✓

Do

  • Use accurate product information.
  • Use clear and honest images.
  • Promote products you can fulfil.
  • Check your pricing before promoting.
  • Understand your target customer.
  • Test and improve your product listings.
  • Monitor advertising performance when advertising tools become available.
  • Keep your seller information up to date.
×

Don't

  • Do not use misleading product images.
  • Do not make false claims about products.
  • Do not advertise products you cannot supply.
  • Do not hide important product information.
  • Do not deliberately mislead shoppers.
  • Do not assume advertising guarantees sales.
  • Do not ignore your product page quality.
  • Do not advertise without understanding what you are trying to achieve.

AVOID THESE MISTAKES

Common Advertising Mistakes Sellers Make

01

Advertising a Weak Listing

Paying for visibility does not fix poor images, unclear descriptions or confusing product information. Improve the listing first.

02

Promoting Everything

A focused strategy can be more useful than trying to promote every product in your store.

03

Ignoring Customer Experience

Advertising may bring attention, but poor communication, fulfilment or delivery can damage the shopping experience.

04

Expecting Instant Results

Advertising should be approached as a marketing tool, not as an automatic guarantee of sales.

SELLER QUESTIONS

Advertising FAQ

What is NEOSTORE advertising?

NEOSTORE advertising will provide sellers with promotional opportunities designed to help give selected products additional visibility within the marketplace.

Is advertising available now?

Advertising tools are currently coming soon. Sellers can use this time to improve their product listings and prepare their stores.

Will advertising guarantee sales?

No. Advertising can help increase product visibility, but sales depend on many factors, including product quality, pricing, demand, presentation, customer trust and fulfilment.

Should I advertise every product?

Not necessarily. A focused strategy can be more effective. Consider promoting products that have strong listings, competitive pricing, reliable availability and a clear customer audience.

What should I do before advertising starts?

Improve your seller profile, product photos, titles, descriptions, pricing, inventory accuracy and customer experience. Being prepared means you can take advantage of promotional opportunities when they become available.

Where will advertising appear?

The specific advertising placements and available promotional formats will be communicated when NEOSTORE advertising tools are launched.
