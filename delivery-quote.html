ZONGO Marketplace Delivery Service

Marketplace delivery options can connect seller pickup with buyer delivery.

How It Works

Where available, a delivery request may arrange collection from the seller and delivery to the buyer without requiring the seller to independently arrange a courier.

Delivery Quote

Delivery charges may depend on pickup location, destination, parcel size or weight, service type, distance, courier availability, and other applicable charges.

Door-to-Door

Where supported, the parcel can be collected from the seller location and delivered to the buyer address.

Urban and Rural Coverage

Coverage may include cities, towns, and selected rural communities. Remote destinations may have different prices, routes, timelines, or availability.

Seller Pickup

Sellers must package the correct order securely and make it ready for pickup at the stated location.

Buyer Delivery

Buyers should provide a complete address and remain reachable for delivery coordination.

Estimated Times

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Delays may result from traffic, weather, security, failed attempts, address problems, holidays, or courier capacity.

Failed Delivery

Additional action or charges may apply where delivery fails because of an incorrect address, unavailable recipient, refusal, or other recipient/sender issue.

Restricted Goods

Products restricted by law, courier rules, payment rules, or marketplace policy may not be shipped.
