NEOSTORE Promotions

How marketplace promotions work for buyers and sellers.

What Is a Promotion?

A promotion is a limited offer that may reduce a product price or provide another qualifying benefit.

Promotion Types

Possible formats include seller discounts, marketplace campaigns, percentage-off, fixed-amount, quantity-based, category, and limited-time offers.

Promotion Terms

Each offer should state eligibility, dates, participating products, minimum purchase requirements, limits, and exclusions.

Buyer Use

Review the terms, add eligible items, enter a code if required, and confirm the discount appears before payment.

Seller Participation

Use approved promotional tools and ensure prices, stock, dates, and eligibility are accurate.

No Misleading Promotions

Do not advertise unavailable, expired, or misleading discounts or inaccurate reference prices.

Campaign Changes

Promotions may be modified, paused, or ended according to their published terms and applicable law.
