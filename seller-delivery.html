Seller Delivery Guide

Steps for preparing marketplace orders for reliable pickup and delivery.

1. Confirm the Order

Check the product, quantity, variation, buyer information, and order status.

2. Prepare the Product

Inspect the product and include all required accessories and documentation.

3. Package Securely

Use packaging suitable for the item and protect fragile or easily damaged products.

4. Handover

Follow the delivery instructions and make the parcel available at the required pickup location and time.

5. Verify Pickup

Use the available pickup confirmation process and do not hand a parcel to an unidentified person claiming to be a courier.

6. Tracking

Use order tracking information in the seller dashboard and retain tracking records where provided.

7. Failed Pickup

Follow the rescheduling or support process if pickup cannot be completed.

8. Delivery Problems

Report missing, damaged, delayed, or incorrectly delivered parcels through official support.
